# R-NB-006 — Seeding precedes randomness in notebooks

**Category:** Notebooks  
**Severity:** low  
**Weight:** 2

## Why it matters

Drawing before deterministic seeding makes notebook results depend on prior
RNG state and can cause reruns to differ; it does not establish that every
rerun will differ.

## Suppressing

Inline, on the reported line: `# adduce: ignore=R-NB-006`  
Project-wide, in `adduce.toml` or `[tool.adduce]`: `ignore = ["R-NB-006"]`

Suppressed findings still appear in reports, marked as ignored.
