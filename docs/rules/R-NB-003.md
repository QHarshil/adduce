# R-NB-003 — Hidden-state risk (gaps in execution counts)

**Category:** Notebooks  
**Severity:** low  
**Weight:** 2

## Why it matters

Execution-count gaps can indicate out-of-order execution, deleted cells, or
other hidden kernel state. They are a review signal, not proof that committed
code was removed or edited.

## Suppressing

Inline, on the reported line: `# adduce: ignore=R-NB-003`  
Project-wide, in `adduce.toml` or `[tool.adduce]`: `ignore = ["R-NB-003"]`

Suppressed findings still appear in reports, marked as ignored.
