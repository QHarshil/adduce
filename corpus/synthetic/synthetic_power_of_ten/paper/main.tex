\section{Training}

We train with AdamW, setting the learning rate to $10^{-4}$ and the weight
decay to $10^{-4}$. We use a learning rate drop after 400 epochs. For
fine-tuning we decay the learning rate by a factor of 10 at epoch 30.

We report results after 300 epochs on 8 GPUs. The head uses a learning rate
of 1$\times$10$^{-4}$. We linearly warm up the learning rate from $10^{-6}$ to
$10^{-4}$, then decay the learning rate to $10^{-5}$ with a cosine schedule.
